Skills

Every skill as the generator reads it: what it causes, what it needs, which engines it feeds, what it clashes with – next to the wiki facts it is based on. Badges show where each trait comes from.

Friction

Anti-synergies from the state model: a skill needs, creates or destroys your own state – health, enchantments, adrenaline, energy, attacking, moving. Soft friction lowers the score (strongly at low weirdness, mildly at high weirdness) and is shown on the card. Hard friction never appears – unless Full Bravery rolls “Self-sabotage allowed”.
